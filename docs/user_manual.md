## 1. Getting Started — Register an Account

1. Go to the DriftDater home page.
2. Click **Sign Up** in the navigation menu.
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Email** — must be a valid email address
   - **Username** — your unique display name
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Date of Birth** — used to display your age
   - **Gender** — select your gender identity
   - **Looking For** — who you want to match with (Male / Female / Any)
   - **Password** — minimum 8 characters
4. Click **Sign Up**.
5. You will be redirected to your dashboard on success.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Click **Login** in the navigation menu.
2. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Login**.
4. You will be taken to your personal dashboard.

> If you forget your password, contact the support team *(or use the password reset option if enabled)*.

---

## 3. Setting Up Your Profile

After registering, complete your profile so other users can find you:

1. From the dashboard, click **Edit Profile**.
2. Fill in or update:
   - **Bio / Description** — tell others about yourself
   - **Location** — your city (e.g. Kingston, Jamaica)
   - **Interests / Hobbies** — select at least 3 from the list
   - **Profile Photo** — upload a JPG or PNG image (max 5MB)
   - **Profile Visibility** — set to **Public** (visible to all) or **Private** (hidden)
3. Click **Save** to update your profile.

> The more complete your profile, the better your match quality!

---

## 4. Browsing Potential Matches

1. Click **Dashboard** in the navigation bar.
2. Scroll to the **Browse Potential Matches** section.
3. Each profile card shows:
   - Name and age
   - Location and bio snippet
   - Match Score (%) — how compatible you are
4. For each profile, choose:
   - 💚 **Like** — you are interested in this person
   - ⚪ **Pass** — skip this profile
5. If they also Like you back, a **Mutual Match** is created!

---

## 5. Your Matches

1. Click **Matches** in the navigation bar.
2. All your mutual matches appear here.
3. Each match card shows the person's name, age, bio, and a **Message** button.
4. Click **Message** to open a conversation.

> You can only message users who have mutually matched with you.

---

## 6. Messaging

1. From the **Matches** page, click the **Message** button on any match.
2. The messaging screen shows:
   - The conversation history on the right
   - A text input box at the bottom
3. Type your message and click **Send**.
4. Messages are saved permanently and visible every time you open the chat.
5. Timestamps are shown on all messages.

---

## 7. Search & Filters

1. From the **Dashboard**, locate the **Browse Potential Matches** section.
2. Use the filter bar at the top to narrow results:
   - **Search by name or bio** — text search
   - **Age range** — select from the dropdown (e.g. 20–30)
   - **Filter by location** — type a city name
   - **Show Interest Filters** — toggle to filter by specific interests
3. Click **Reset Filters** to clear all active filters.
4. Use the **Sort** options to order results by:
   - **Newest** — most recently joined users first
   - **Best Match** — highest match score first

---

## 8. Favourites / Bookmarks

1. On any profile card, click the ⭐ **Favourite** icon to save a profile.
2. Access your saved profiles by clicking **Favourites** in the menu.
3. To remove a bookmark, click the ⭐ icon again on the profile.

---

## 9. Editing Your Profile

1. Click **Dashboard** to view your profile summary.
2. Click **Edit Profile**.
3. Update any fields you wish to change.
4. To change your profile photo, click the photo area and upload a new image.
5. Click **Save** when done.

---

## 10. Logging Out

1. Click **Logout** in the top navigation bar.
2. Your session will end and you will be returned to the home page.

---

## Tips for a Great Experience

- **Complete your profile fully** — users with photos and detailed bios get more matches.
- **Select varied interests** — the more interests you list, the more people you can match with.
- **Check back regularly** — new users join every day and your match list updates automatically.
- **Be respectful** — keep all messages friendly and appropriate.
